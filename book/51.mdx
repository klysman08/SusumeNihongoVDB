## 1. From action to outcome
<PatternCompare client:load title="Result or evidential basis?" items={[
  { pattern: "V-た結果／Nの結果", meaning: "as a result of", use: "Presents the outcome after an action, process, or investigation has run its course.", example: "話し合った結果、計画を延期することになった。", translation: "As a result of the discussion, the plan was postponed.", register: "neutral–formal" },
  { pattern: "plain form + ことから", meaning: "from the fact that; based on", use: "Treats a fact, feature, or observation as evidence for a name, judgement, or inference.", example: "形が星に似ていることから、この名前が付けられた。", translation: "It was given this name because its shape resembles a star.", register: "written" },
  { pattern: "plain form + わけだ", meaning: "that explains it; naturally it follows", use: "The speaker reaches or confirms a logical conclusion from information already presented.", example: "十年日本に住んでいたんですか。日本語が自然なわけですね。", translation: "You lived in Japan for ten years?   { pattern: "plain form + ということだ", meaning: "that means; the information is that", use: "Restates the implication or reported content in a concise form.", example: "返事がないということは、まだ決めていないということだ。", translation: "No reply means that they have not decided yet.", register: "neutral" }
]} />
<CommonMistake title="わけだ is not a vague filler"><p>Use <strong>わけだ</strong> when a conclusion becomes understandable from stated evidence. It does not simply mean “because,” and it should not introduce an unrelated opinion.</p></CommonMistake>
