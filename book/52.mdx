  { pattern: "plain + ようだ／みたいだ", meaning: "it appears; it seems", use: "Inference from evidence. ようだ is neutral/written; みたいだ is conversational.", example: "電気がついている。まだ誰かいるようだ。", translation: "The lights are on. It seems someone is still there.", register: "inference" },
  { pattern: "plain + らしい", meaning: "apparently; characteristically", use: "Often reports indirect information; with a noun it can also mean 'typical of'.", example: "田中さんは来月退職するらしい。", translation: "Apparently, Mr Tanaka will retire next month.", register: "indirect" }

]} />
<CommonMistake title="The two そうだ patterns connect differently"><p><strong>おいしそうだ</strong> is a visual judgement and drops the adjective's final い. <strong>おいしいそうだ</strong> reports that somebody said it is delicious. Do not mix their formations.</p></CommonMistake>
