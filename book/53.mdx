<CommonMistake title="A sufficient condition is not always realistic"><p><strong>お金さえあれば幸せだ</strong> claims money alone is sufficient for happiness. Because <strong>さえ〜ば</strong> excludes other requirements, use it only when that strong claim is intended.</p></CommonMistake>
