## JLPT N3 Grammar Book Structure

This book can be organised as a guided progression from upper-beginner grammar to intermediate Japanese. Each chapter should include explanations, example sentences, short dialogues, exercises, reading practice, and review activities.

# Part 1 — Strengthening the Foundations

## Chapter 1 — Reviewing Verb Forms

Topics:

* Dictionary form
* ます form
* て form
* た form
* ない form
* Potential form
* Volitional form
* Imperative and prohibition forms

Learning goal:

Understand how verb forms are created and recognise them quickly in longer sentences.

---

## Chapter 2 — Transitive and Intransitive Verbs

Topics:

* 他動詞 and 自動詞
* ドアを開ける vs ドアが開く
* 電気を消す vs 電気が消える
* Describing intentional and natural changes
* Common transitive–intransitive verb pairs

Grammar patterns:

* ～てある
* ～ている
* ～ておく

Learning goal:

Explain whether someone performed an action or whether something changed by itself.

---

## Chapter 3 — Giving and Receiving

Topics:

* あげる
* くれる
* もらう
* ～てあげる
* ～てくれる
* ～てもらう

Additional concepts:

* Speaker perspective
* Social relationships
* When ～てあげる may sound inappropriate
* Polite forms such as ～ていただく

Learning goal:

Describe favours, help, and actions performed for other people.

# Part 2 — Time, Actions, and Progress

* ～ていく

* Preparing in advance

* Regret or accidental actions

* Changes moving toward or away from the present

Example:

“I will reserve the hotel before the trip.”

## Recommended Lesson Structure

Each chapter can follow the same format:

1. **Context dialogue**
   A natural conversation that introduces the grammar.

2. **Grammar explanation**
   Meaning, formation, usage, and tone.

3. **Sentence formation**
   Connections with verbs, nouns, い-adjectives, and な-adjectives.

4. **Comparison section**
   Differences between similar patterns.

5. **Example sentences**
   Examples from daily life, school, work, travel, and JLPT contexts.

6. **Common mistakes**
   Incorrect sentences with explanations.

7. **Guided practice**
   Fill-in-the-blank and sentence transformation exercises.

8. **Communication practice**
   Short speaking or writing activities.

9. **Reading practice**
   A paragraph using the chapter grammar.

10. **Review flashcards**
    Grammar meaning, formation, and one contextual example.

A practical book would contain around **50–55 chapters**, divided into **18 parts**, with approximately **150–200 grammar patterns and related expressions**. This gives learners enough structure to move progressively from N4 foundations toward confident N3 comprehension.
