## 1. A reason has a tone

<ParticleCompare client:load title="Choosing a reason expression" items={[
  { label: "〜から", role: "direct, personal explanation", example: "今日は疲れたから、早く寝る。", note: "Natural in conversation; can sound firm when giving a reason for your own decision." },
  { label: "〜ので", role: "softer, more considerate explanation", example: "少し遅れますので、先に始めてください。", note: "Often preferred when speaking politely or making a request." },
  { label: "〜ため", role: "formal cause or official explanation", example: "大雨のため、イベントは延期です。", note: "Common in notices, announcements, and written Japanese." },
  { label: "〜おかげで / 〜せいで", role: "positive help / negative blame", example: "先生のおかげで合格できた。", note: "Choose according to whether the result is welcome or unwanted." }
]} />

## 2. Credit, blame, and responsibility

<GrammarBox client:load pattern="N のおかげで / N のせいで" meaning="Thanks to N (positive result) / because of N (negative result or blame)." formation="Noun + のおかげで / のせいで. A clause can also come before おかげで or せいで." examples={[
  { japanese: "友達が手伝ってくれたおかげで、レポートが間に合った。", romaji: "tomodachi ga tetsudatte kureta okage de, repooto ga maniatta.", translation: "Thanks to my friend's help, I finished the report in time." },
  { japanese: "渋滞のせいで、会議に遅れてしまった。", romaji: "juutai no sei de, kaigi ni okurete shimatta.", translation: "Because of the traffic, I ended up late for the meeting." },
  { japanese: "彼のせいで失敗した、と決めつけないほうがいい。", romaji: "kare no sei de shippai shita, to kimetsukenai hou ga ii.", translation: "It is better not to jump to the conclusion that you failed because of him." }
]} />

<CommonMistake title="Do not use せいで for your own neutral explanation">
  <p><strong>電車が遅れたせいで、遅れました</strong> is possible, but it carries blame. For a neutral apology, use <strong>電車が遅れたので、遅れました</strong>. Reserve <strong>せいで</strong> for a clearly unwelcome result.</p>
</CommonMistake>

## 4. Guided practice

Choose the best expression, then explain the tone.

1. You tell a friend: “I am going home because I am tired.”
2. You politely ask a teacher to begin without you because you will be late.
3. A station notice: “Service is suspended due to strong winds.”
4. You thank a classmate: “Thanks to you, I understood the assignment.”
5. You complain that a noisy neighbour caused your poor sleep.

## 5. Reading practice: a school announcement

> 本日の校外授業は、強風のため中止になりました。代わりに、三時から教室で映画を見ます。急な変更なので、全員に連絡できない可能性があります。友達が来ていない場合は、この知らせを伝えてください。先生方の準備のおかげで、授業の内容は来週も続けられます。

**Reading check:** Find (1) the formal cause, (2) the reason the school may not reach everyone, and (3) the positive result credited to the teachers.

Translation: “Today's outdoor lesson has been cancelled because of strong winds. Instead, we will watch a film in the classroom from three o'clock. Because this is a sudden change, it may not be possible to contact everyone. If a friend has not come, please pass on this notice. Thanks to the teachers' preparation, we can continue the lesson content next week.”

## 6. Communication and flashcard review

**Speak or write:** explain one changed plan using `〜ので`, one formal notice using `〜ため`, and one positive result using `〜おかげで`.

**Review flashcards:** `〜から` → direct personal reason; `〜ので` → softer explanation; `〜ため` → formal objective cause; `〜おかげで` → positive result; `〜せいで` → negative result/blame.
