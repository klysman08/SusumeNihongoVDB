<SectionIntro level="N3">Formal notices and articles often compress relationships into に-expressions. Instead of translating every one as “about,” identify whether the noun is a topic, target, viewpoint, cause, means, agent, or setting.</SectionIntro>

<CommonMistake title="にとって cannot mark a recipient"><p>Say <strong>学生に対して説明する</strong> for “explain to students.” <strong>学生にとって説明する</strong> is ungrammatical for that meaning because にとって establishes a viewpoint, not a target.</p></CommonMistake>
