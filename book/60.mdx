<CommonMistake title="がち and 気味 do not describe the same time scale"><p><strong>忘れがちだ</strong> describes repeated forgetting. <strong>忘れ気味だ</strong> describes a recent or mild tendency. Choose according to whether the focus is recurrence or current condition.</p></CommonMistake>
