## 1. Strength and status of a plan

<PatternCompare client:load title="How settled is the plan?" items={[
  { pattern: "V-る／V-ないつもりだ", meaning: "firm present intention", use: "States what the subject currently intends.   { pattern: "V-ようと思う", meaning: "a decision or thought forming now", use: "Often softer and less settled than つもり. The volitional form highlights the speaker's will.", example: "今夜、先生に相談しようと思います。", translation: "I think I’ll consult the teacher tonight.", register: "forming intention" },
  { pattern: "V-ることを考えている", meaning: "be considering", use: "The idea remains under consideration and may change.", example: "来年、大学院へ進むことを考えています。", translation: "I am considering going to graduate school next year.", register: "tentative" },

<CommonMistake title="予定 is not simply another word for hope"><p><strong>日本へ行く予定です</strong> suggests an arranged trip. If it is only an idea, use <strong>行こうと思っています</strong> or <strong>行くことを考えています</strong>.</p></CommonMistake>
