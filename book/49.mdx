<SectionIntro level="N3">Purpose expressions reveal the relationship between an action and its goal. The decisive question is not whether English says “in order to,” but whether the subject can directly control the desired action or result.</SectionIntro>

<CommonMistake title="Potential verbs normally point to ように"><p>Say <strong>日本語が話せるように練習する</strong>, not <strong>話せるために</strong>, when the goal is gaining an ability. Use <strong>日本で働くために日本語を勉強する</strong> when the goal is a deliberate future action.</p></CommonMistake>
