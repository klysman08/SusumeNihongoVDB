## 4. Cultural Insight: Japanese Work Culture (日本の仕事文化)

The Japanese workplace is famous for its unique customs, many of which are reflected in the kanji we've studied.

### Zangyou (残業) — Overtime
The word **残業 (zangyou)** literally means "remaining work." In many Japanese companies, it is common to stay late until the task is finished or until the superior leaves. While this is changing with "Work-Life Balance" initiatives, the concept of dedication to the group remains strong.

### Nomikai (飲み会) — Drinking Parties
Socializing after work is considered a part of the job in many traditional companies. These parties, called **飲み会 (nomikai)**, are where employees can relax (to an extent) and speak more freely with their bosses. It's often said that "Nomunication" (Nomi + Communication) is essential for career building.

### Punctuality (時間厳守)
Being on time is non-negotiable. If a meeting starts at 9:00 AM, being there at 9:00 AM is considered "late." Most employees arrive at least 10 minutes early to be ready the moment the clock strikes.
