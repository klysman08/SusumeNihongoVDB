<SectionIntro level="N3">Reading speed improves when you stop treating conjunctions as decoration. They tell you how the next sentence changes the argument. Long sentences become manageable when you bracket modifiers and wait for the final main predicate.</SectionIntro>

<CommonMistake title="つまり does not add new evidence"><p><strong>つまり</strong> should restate what the previous information means. If the next sentence adds another independent reason, use <strong>さらに</strong> or another additive connector.</p></CommonMistake>
