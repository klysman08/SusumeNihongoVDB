## 1. Past Tense for Nouns & な-Adjectives

Nouns and な-adjectives are "lazy" conjugators. They don't change themselves; they rely entirely on the **Copula (です)** to change into the past tense.

- **Affirmative:** [Noun] **でした (deshita)**
- **Negative:** [Noun] **じゃありませんでした (ja arimasen deshita)**

  pattern="[Noun] ＋ でした"
  meaning="It was [Noun]."
  formation="Noun + でした / じゃありませんでした"
  examples={[
    { japanese: "きのう は やすみ でした。", romaji: "Kinou wa yasumi deshita.", translation: "Yesterday was a holiday." },
    { japanese: "あめ じゃありませんでした。", romaji: "Ame ja arimasen deshita.", translation: "It was not rain." },
    { japanese: "にほんご の しけん は かんたん でした。", romaji: "Nihongo no shiken wa kantan deshita.", translation: "The Japanese exam was easy." }
  ]}
/>

<ExampleSentence client:load 
  japanese="こどものとき、しずか でした。"
  romaji="Kodomo no toki, shizuka deshita."
  translation="When I was a child, I was quiet."
/>
<ExampleSentence client:load 
  japanese="パーティー は にぎやか でした か。"
  romaji="Paatii wa nigiyaka deshita ka?"

  japanese="せんしゅう は ひま じゃありませんでした。"

## 2. Past Tense for い-Adjectives

Unlike nouns, **い-adjectives** change their own endings. To make them past tense, you must drop the final **い** and add **かった (katta)**.

<GrammarBox client:load 
  pattern="[Adj Root] ＋ かった です"
  meaning="It was [Adjective]."
  formation="Drop い + add かった"
  examples={[
    { japanese: "あつかった です。", romaji: "Atsukatta desu.", translation: "It was hot." },
    { japanese: "おもしろくなかった です。", romaji: "Omoshirokunai desu ➔ Omoshirokunakatta desu.", translation: "It was not interesting." },
    { japanese: "てんき は よかった です。", romaji: "Tenki wa yokatta desu.", translation: "The weather was good. (ii ➔ yokatta)" }
  ]}
/>

<ExampleSentence client:load 
  japanese="きのう の りょうり は おいしかった です。"
  romaji="Kinou no ryouri wa oishikatta desu."
  translation="Yesterday's meal was delicious."
/>
<ExampleSentence client:load 
  japanese="テスト は むずかしくなかった です。"
  romaji="Tesuto wa muzukashikunakatta desu."
  translation="The test was not difficult."
/>
<ExampleSentence client:load 
  japanese="きょねん の ふゆ は とても さむかった です。"
  romaji="Kyonen no fuyu wa totemo samukatta desu."
  translation="Last year's winter was very cold."
/>

## 3. Past Tense for Verbs (ます-Form)

For polite verbs, the conjugation is consistent across all groups. You simply change the **ます** ending.

- **Affirmative:** 〜**ました (mashita)**
- **Negative:** 〜**ませんでした (masen deshita)**

<GrammarBox client:load 
  pattern="[Verb Stem] ＋ ました"
  meaning="Performed the action in the past."
  formation="Stem + ました / ませんでした"
  examples={[
    { japanese: "すし を たべました。", romaji: "Sushi o tabemashita.", translation: "I ate sushi." },
    { japanese: "どこ へ も いきませんでした。", romaji: "Doko e mo ikimasen deshita.", translation: "I didn't go anywhere." },
    { japanese: "だれ と あいました か。", romaji: "Dare to aimashita ka?", translation: "Who did you meet?" }
  ]}
/>

<ExampleSentence client:load 
  japanese="きのう は じゅうじ に ねました。"
  romaji="Kinou wa juu-ji ni nemashita."
  translation="Yesterday, I went to sleep at 10:00."
/>
<ExampleSentence client:load 
  japanese="あさごはん を たべませんでした。"
  romaji="Asagohan o tabemasen deshita."
  translation="I didn't eat breakfast."
/>

## 4. Cultural Note: New Year Reflections (Oshougatsu)

In Japan, the New Year (**Oshougatsu**) is the most important holiday. It is a time for **reflection** on the past year and making wishes for the future.

- **Hatsumode (初詣):** The first shrine or temple visit of the year. People thank the gods for the past year's safety and pray for a good new year.
- **Osechi-ryouri (おせち料理):** Special traditional foods eaten during the first three days. Each ingredient has a symbolic meaning related to health, fertility, or harvest.
- **Nengajou (年賀状):** New Year's greeting cards. People write about what **happened** in the past year and express hopes for the next.

**Language Tip:** When reflecting, you will use the past tense constantly!
- *Kyonen wa tanoshikatta desu.* (Last year was fun.)
- *Takusan benkyou shimashita.* (I studied a lot.)
