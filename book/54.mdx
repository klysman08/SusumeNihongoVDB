<CommonMistake title="Do not soften a complaint with くせに"><p><strong>新人なのに詳しい</strong> expresses surprise. <strong>新人のくせに偉そうだ</strong> attacks the person for acting contrary to the speaker's expectation. The second form is deliberately harsh.</p></CommonMistake>
